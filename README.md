# Featured Projects

## 1. AI Voice Receptionist

**Problem:** Businesses can miss potential customers when calls come in after hours or when staff are unavailable.

**Solution:** Built a 24/7 AI receptionist capable of answering questions, collecting information, and scheduling appointments.

### System Flow

```text
Incoming Call
      ↓
AI Voice Agent
      ↓
Understand Customer Intent
      ↓
Answer Question
      ↓
Collect Information
      ↓
Book Appointment
      ↓
CRM / Calendar
      ↓
Customer Confirmation
      ↓
Business Owner Notification
```

### Technical Challenges

* Creating reliable business-specific responses
* Preventing the agent from providing incorrect information
* Connecting the agent to appointment scheduling
* Handling human-transfer requests
* Testing different customer conversations
* Designing workflows around different booking scenarios
